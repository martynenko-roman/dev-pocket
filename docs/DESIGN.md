# DevPocket design plan

- Archetype: menu bar extra in window style, nonactivating toast, Settings window. No Dock icon.
- Information architecture: one panel; Settings tabs General and Shortcuts. General holds Keep Awake, System, Sound, Mouse, and Startup.
- Command map: cup toggles Keep Awake; its context menu selects duration or turns off. Four labeled buttons toggle hidden files, desktop icons, dark mode, and Dock auto-hide. Sound menu selects output/input. Gear and Command-comma open Settings; Quit and Command-Q terminate. Four optional global shortcuts have no defaults, as specified.
- Empty states: no audio devices says No devices; running-app picker has Choose an App; permission failures stay beside the relevant control.
- Feeling: quick and confident. Controls report real state, show pending work, and explain failures.
- Icon concepts: pocket with a tool on indigo; two sliding switches on teal; simple toolbox on blue. Chosen: pocket with tool, easiest to recognize small. Original filled vector shapes, no SF Symbols.
